## What's New in v1.0.10: The "Smart Autopilot" Update

We've completely overhauled how the AI interacts with your customers. It's no longer just a simple bot—it's a highly intelligent, context-aware digital assistant capable of sounding human and knowing when to hand over the reins.

### 🧠 Smarter, More Human AI

- **Smart Escalation (Decision Stop):** The AI now knows when it's time to stop talking. If a customer needs a real human, the AI automatically pauses the conversation and lets you take over seamlessly.
- **Personalized Greetings:** Build instant rapport. The AI now automatically greets your prospects by their actual names (e.g., "Halo Budi, ...").
- **Human Simulation:** We've tweaked the engine to make the AI type and behave more like a real person, eliminating "bot fatigue" from your customers.
- **Total Control (Stop Keywords):** Define specific trigger words (like "admin" or "bantuan") that will instantly pause the AI for that exact chat.
- **Group & Archive Protection:** Keep your groups completely clean. You can now automatically block the AI from replying to Group chats and Archived conversations.

### 📅 Campaign Scheduling

- **Schedule While You Sleep:** Stop waiting around to hit "send." You can now set exact dates and times for your Blast and Warmer campaigns to begin. Set it, forget it, and let the leads roll in.

### 🗺️ Prompt Templates & Deep Scraping

- **Prompt Library:** Save your best-performing AI instructions as templates. Switch between different AI personas instantly without retyping everything.
- **Advanced Scraping Base:** Massive underlying improvements to the Google Maps Scraper architecture for deeper, more reliable B2B lead generation.

### 🛡️ Iron-Clad Stability

- **Bulletproof Database Upgrades:** A completely rewritten migration engine ensures that every time you update WazapSuite, your database, WhatsApp sessions, and precious chat history remain 100% safe, intact, and automatically upgraded in the background.

## 🔄 How to Update

**Already using an older version?**

1. Download the new `WazapSuite.Setup.1.0.10.exe` above.
2. Run the installer.
3. Relax. The installer will seamlessly overwrite your old version while **keeping all your data, WhatsApp sessions, and history perfectly safe**.

_Note: Starting from v1.0.0, the app automatically checks this repository for new updates on startup and will notify you when future versions are ready._
